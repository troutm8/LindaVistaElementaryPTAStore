# Volunteer guide: running the Linda Vista PTA online store

This is for the PTA volunteer who packs and delivers online orders. No computer skills
beyond using a website are needed. A full week of orders usually takes one packing session.

**The board:** https://linda-vista-pta-store.vercel.app/admin
**Login:** any username, and the volunteer password from the PTA president.

Tip: on a phone, open the board in Safari or Chrome and tap Share → "Add to Home Screen"
so it works like an app.

## How an order moves

Every order goes through the same four steps. The parent sees the same steps on their
order page, and they get an email at two of them (marked ✉).

| Step | What it means | Who moves it |
|---|---|---|
| **Paid** ✉ | A parent paid online. The order is waiting for you. | Automatic |
| **Packed** | You bagged it and labelled it. | You, on the board |
| **Ready** ✉ | The bag is at the front office, or handed to the teacher. The parent gets a "your bag is ready" email. | You, on the board |
| **Picked up** | The family has it. | You, or the office |

Orders show up on the board the moment Square confirms the payment. Nobody has to approve them.

## A packing session, start to finish

### 1. Print the packing list

Board → **Packing list**. The "To pack" tab shows everything waiting.

- The **Pull from stock** section at the top totals every item across all orders, so you
  can pull everything from the shelves in one pass.
- Below it, every bag is grouped by where it goes: **Front office pickup** first, then each
  **teacher** in alphabetical order. Each line has a checkbox for ticking on paper.

Press **Print**. One sheet is usually enough.

### 2. Bag each order

For each line on the list:

1. Put the items in a bag.
2. Print the label: on the board, find the order and tap **Print label**. The label shows the
   family name in big letters, the student and teacher, the order number, and the contents.
   For send-home orders it says **Walk to: [teacher]**. Tape it to the bag.
3. On the board, tap **Mark packed**.

If the parent wrote a note (it shows in orange on the card and on the list), read it. It's
usually "the blue one please" or "my kid is in the afternoon class."

### 3. Deliver

Packing list → **To deliver** tab shows only the packed bags, grouped the same way.

- **Front office** bags go on the PTA shelf in the office.
- **Send-home** bags go to the teacher's classroom. Teacher mailboxes are too small for a
  bag, so walk them over.

Once a bag is where it belongs, tap **Mark ready (emails family)** on the board. That
sends the parent the "your bag is ready" email, so only do it once the bag is actually there.

### 4. Close out

When a family collects an office bag, tap **Mark picked up**. The office can do this too if
they have the password. Send-home orders can be marked picked up right after you mark
them ready, since the teacher handles the rest.

Made a mistake? Every step has a "back" button (for example **Back to "to pack"** on a
packed order). Nothing is final except cancelling.

## Cancelling an order and refunds

Use this when you can't fill an order: sold out, wrong size with no replacement, or the
parent asks.

1. On the board, find the order and tap **Cancel order**.
2. The next page tells you exactly what will happen: how much goes back to the parent's
   card through Square, which items go back into stock, and that the parent gets an email.
3. Optionally type a reason. It goes into the parent's email, so keep it friendly
   ("Sorry, we sold out of youth small!").
4. Tap **Refund … and cancel**.

The refund goes straight back to the card the parent paid with through Square, usually
within 5 to 10 business days. You do not need to do anything in the Square app. If Square
refuses the refund for some reason, the order is left alone and the page tells you why.

Cancelled orders stay visible on the **Cancelled** tab with the refund amount.

Picked-up orders can't be cancelled from the board. If a family returns something after
pickup, refund it in the Square app the same way you would for an in-person sale.

## Stock

Board → **Stock**. Every size and color shows its count. The store shows "Sold out" to
parents when a count reaches zero, and nobody can order more than what's there.

- After a delivery of new shirts, add them here.
- If the shelf count is off, fix it here. Type the new number and tap **Save** on that row.
- "Low" appears at 5 or fewer.

Paid orders reduce stock automatically. Cancelled orders put it back automatically.

## For the treasurer

Board → **Orders CSV** downloads a spreadsheet with one row per order, including the
Square order and payment IDs for matching against the Square dashboard and the bank.
**Items CSV** is one row per item, for "how many youth smalls did we sell."

Both open in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets. Times are Pacific.

## Emails the parent gets

| When | Email |
|---|---|
| They pay | Receipt with the order number, items, and a link to track the order |
| You mark ready | "Your bag is ready," saying where it is |
| You cancel | "Your order was cancelled," with the refund amount and your reason |

Square also sends its own payment receipt separately.

Note: until the PTA sets up an email address for the store, these emails are not actually
sent. The order page link still works for parents, and the board works normally.

## Questions parents ask

**"Where's my order?"** Ask for the order number (LV-0012, on their receipt). Find it on
the board; the card shows the status and when it changed.

**"Can I change my order?"** There's no edit. Cancel it (they get the refund) and ask them
to order again.

**"I never got an email."** Check spam, and check the email on the order card for typos.
The order page link works even without the email.

**"Can I pay at the office instead?"** Not through this site. Point them to the in-person
store days.

## If something looks wrong

- Orders not appearing, or the site is down: tell the PTA president, who will contact
  whoever maintains the site.
- A payment shows in Square but not on the board: wait a minute and refresh. If it still
  isn't there, note the Square payment ID and report it.
- Never share the volunteer password in a group chat. It can be changed, but ask first.
